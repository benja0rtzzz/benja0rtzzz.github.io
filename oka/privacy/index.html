Oka

Privacy policy

Effective October 3, 2026

In short: Oka runs on your iPhone, has no servers or accounts, and keeps what it reads on your phone.

What Oka is

Oka is a personal-finance app for iPhone. It tracks your spending against goals you set and suggests what to do when you drift. It only reads and suggests: it never signs in to a bank and never moves money.

What Oka reads

  • Purchases you add by hand.
  • Apple Pay purchases, when you set up a Shortcuts automation that passes Oka the card, merchant and amount of each tap.
  • Gmail, if you connect it. Oka asks Google for read-only access (the gmail.readonly permission) and downloads only messages from the bank senders you pick in the app. Google’s permission covers your whole mailbox, because Google offers no narrower one. Oka limits itself to your chosen senders, and never changes, sends or deletes mail.

What Oka keeps, and where

Oka keeps the details it needs to track your goals, such as the amount, merchant and date of each purchase, along with your goals, its checks and its suggestions. All of it stays in the app’s own storage on your iPhone. If your iPhone’s backups are on, that storage is part of your device backup, like any app’s.

How Oka reasons

Oka uses Apple’s on-device model, part of Apple Intelligence, to read bank emails and to reason about your goals. The model runs on your iPhone. For this, Oka sends your purchases and emails to no one, Apple included.

What Oka sends

Nothing about you. Oka has no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The only connections it makes are to Google: to let you sign in, and to download the emails from the senders you picked.

Google user data

Oka does not sell, share or transfer data it receives from Google, and no person reads your emails. Oka’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

  • Disconnect Gmail in Oka’s settings at any time.
  • Remove Oka’s access from your Google Account, under Third-party apps and services.
  • Delete Oka from your iPhone to delete everything it stored there.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs under GitHub’s privacy statement.
