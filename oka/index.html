Oka

A personal-finance app for iPhone, in development. It is not on the App Store.

What it does

Oka tracks your spending against goals you set. When a goal is off track, it can suggest an action, which runs only if you approve it. It does not connect to banks or move money.

Where purchases come from

  • Purchases you add by hand.
  • Apple Pay purchases, through a Shortcuts automation you set up.
  • Bank alert emails in Gmail, if you connect it: read-only, and only from the senders you pick.

Where your data stays

On your iPhone. Oka uses Apple’s on-device model and has no servers. The privacy policy has the details.
